Top 5 cyber trends of the past week

1Trusted vendor channels become the way in: ConnectWise, AOMEI and Rank Math

Attackers used a compromised Microsoft 365 credential for ConnectWise Cloud Backup (formerly SkyKick) to sign in to at least 173 customer tenants per Huntress, with activity peaking October 15 and, in at least one tenant, an app granting itself Global Administrator; ConnectWise disabled seven application identities and suspended Cloud Backup, SaaS Security and Cloud Management while it investigates. The same pattern hit software updates, as Google and ManageEngine tied UNC7017 to brief tampering of AOMEI Backupper's update servers (three windows of 5–10 minutes between May and July) that pushed the CROSSROAD downloader and SHINYROAD backdoor, and Rank Math PRO build 3.0.123 (CVE-2026-111862, CVSS 9.8 per Wordfence) shipped a self-restoring backdoor for about 11 hours on October 15–16.

2Zero-click spyware rides in on targeted ads

Citizen Lab's “AdFection” research says Israeli ad-tech firm Discover-Tech bought real ad placements that served JavaScript only to chosen devices, delivering Candiru spyware to at least 45 Al Jazeera devices, with ads from March to June 2026 (3.77 billion bids, 442 million auctions won) appearing in 75,373 apps and 120,768 websites, though those figures are not infection counts. Google, IAB Europe, Beeswax, Eskimi and Vungle cut off the operator, while Citizen Lab confirmed on October 19 that Serbia's University of Belgrade rector was infected with NSO's Pegasus days before the October 25 election, bringing the 2026 count of spyware-targeted people in Serbia to at least 15.

3Emergency patches for web-stack and virtualization flaws, one already exploited

F5 shipped out-of-band fixes for three critical NGINX bugs (CVE-2026-15176, CVE-2026-97205, CVE-2026-93479) that cause heap overflows or use-after-free in the worker process, Vercel patched two Next.js remote-code-execution flaws (CVSS 9.5; fixed in 16.4.1) and Drupal fixed RCE bugs CVE-2026-108097 and CVE-2026-108099 while warning that exploits could appear within hours or days, with none of these reported as exploited yet. By contrast, attackers are already abusing Collabora's CODE Server app for Nextcloud (CVE-2026-110447, fixed in 26.4.303) to drop PHP web shells, and the Linux kernel flaw CVE-2026-99999 “PwnEscape” (CVSS 9.3) lets a guest VM read host memory, with fixes in stable kernels such as 6.12.113 and 6.18.57.

4Takedowns and prosecutions hit phishing kits, North Korean IT workers and a crime market

On October 14 Microsoft, Health-ISAC, Japan's JC3 and partners disrupted the CoGUI, Haozi and Iron Man System phishing kits sold through the YAM Store (Storm-3119), which sent over 1 billion emails and SMS messages to more than 75,000 organizations using about 17,000 domains. The DOJ announced charges on October 13 against 14 people in a North Korean remote-work scheme (searches of 40-plus Texas residences, about 187 computers seized, work at 70-plus companies), and North Macedonian police with the FBI arrested the suspected administrator of the OLVX credential marketplace, which had more than 1,500 seller accounts.

5ClickFix and fake AI tools trick users into running the malware themselves

Any.Run analyzed 1,258 fake-AI-tool incidents over six months, finding Claude impersonated in 60% and ChatGPT or Codex in 33%, with about 45% involving victims pasting a command and developers' SSH keys, CI/CD tokens and API keys as the main prize. Forescout's Vedere Labs found six servers running the shared ROMULUS ClickFix platform (delivering LegionLoader in a roughly 842 MiB padded archive), and Huntress tied it to attackers abusing a signed Siemens Software Center via DLL sideloading (CVE-2026-110936, CVSS v4 8.4, fixed in 4.0.9.8) to install a remote access trojan.
